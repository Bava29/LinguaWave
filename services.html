Ask Us a Question
Equipment availability and arrangements should be confirmed for each event.
What interpretation services do you offer?

LinguaWave's service information focuses on interpretation equipment, audio solutions and technical coordination for multilingual events. Contact us to discuss your specific requirements and confirm available services.

Which events can request equipment support?

Requirements may be discussed for international conferences, corporate meetings, seminars, workshops and multilingual delegations. Suitability depends on your event's needs.

How do I choose the right equipment?

Start with your audience size, event format, languages required, venue and audio setup. These details help determine which equipment options should be considered.

What information should I provide for an enquiry?

Share your event date, venue or location, expected audience size, languages involved and any known technical requirements.

Can equipment be arranged for different audience sizes?

Equipment quantities should be planned around the expected audience and event format. Confirm the available quantities and suitable options when making your enquiry.

When should I submit my event enquiry?

Contact the team as early as practical so event requirements, equipment availability and venue considerations can be discussed before your event date.
