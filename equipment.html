Questions Before You Book Your Equipment?

Find answers to common questions about interpretation equipment rentals, event preparation and booking.

Still have questions?

Talk to our team about your event requirements.

Contact Our Team
01 What information should I provide for a rental quote?

Share your event date, venue, expected audience size, required languages, event format and the equipment you think you may need. These details help our team discuss a suitable setup and prepare a quote.

02 How many receivers and headphones will I need?

The quantity depends on your expected audience, the number of people who need interpretation and your event arrangements. Share your estimated attendee count so the requirements can be assessed.

03 Can the equipment support multiple languages?

The available languages and simultaneous audio channels depend on the selected system and setup. Tell us how many languages your event requires so compatibility can be confirmed before booking.

04 Are delivery, installation and testing included?

Delivery, installation, equipment testing and collection arrangements may depend on your rental agreement and location. Confirm the included services and any additional charges in your quote.

05 Can I rent equipment for just one day?

Rental duration and availability depend on the equipment and booking schedule. Share your event dates and required rental period to confirm the available options.

06 Is technical support available during the event?

Technical assistance may be arranged depending on your event requirements and rental agreement. Ask our team about setup support, testing and event-day assistance when requesting your quote.

07 How early should I book the equipment?

Contact us as early as possible, particularly for large events or multiple language channels. Availability depends on your dates and equipment requirements, so early confirmation is helpful.

08 What if my event requirements change?

Contact the team as soon as your requirements change. Equipment availability, revised costs and booking amendments will depend on the rental terms and the requested changes.
